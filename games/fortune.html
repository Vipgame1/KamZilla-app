HOW TO PLAY

Match three symbols from left to right on any of the five fixed lines: three rows and two diagonals.

The center symbol on the fourth reel is the active multiplier applied to every winning line.

Garuda is a Wild — it substitutes for any regular symbol. If a Garuda lands on a main reel, that reel expands before lines score.

Extra Bet adds 50% to the stake and increases multiplier paths.
